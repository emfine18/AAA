About the Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC

Antisemitism is not a relic of history — it is a growing threat in our politics, schools, workplaces, and communities. To confront it effectively, we must act.

The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC is dedicated to fighting antisemitism and ensuring that combating antisemitism remains a national priority rooted in America’s core values of democracy, freedom, and respect for all people.

This is personal. Antisemitism affects real people and real lives. Silence is not an option. Together, we can make moral leadership political leadership.
