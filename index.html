About the Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC

Antisemitism is not a relic of history — it is a growing threat in our politics, schools, workplaces, and communities. To confront it effectively, we must act.

The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC is dedicated to fighting antisemitism. It ensures that combating antisemitism remains a national priority rooted in America’s core values of democracy, freedom, and respect for all people. By supporting candidates who take this fight seriously, we help make sure that moral leadership is reflected in political leadership.

But this isn’t only about policy. It’s personal. Antisemitism touches real people, real families, and real lives. This PAC exists because silence is not an option, and statements are not enough. We believe that every person deserves to live free from hate and fear — and that together, we can make that a political reality. Together, we can send a message that antisemitism — and all forms of hate — have no place in our politics or our country.
