About the Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC

Antisemitism is not a relic of history — it is a growing threat in our politics, schools, workplaces, and communities. To confront it effectively, we must act.

The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC is dedicated to fighting antisemitism and ensuring it remains a national priority rooted in America’s core values of democracy, freedom, and respect for all people.

This isn’t only about policy — it’s personal. Silence is not an option. Together, we can send a message that antisemitism and all forms of hate have no place in our politics or our country.
