About the Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC

Antisemitism is not a relic of history — it is a growing threat in our politics, schools, workplaces, and communities. To confront it effectively, we must act.

The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC is dedicated to fighting antisemitism. It ensures that combating antisemitism remains a national priority rooted in America’s core values of democracy, freedom, and respect for all people.

But this isn’t only about policy. It’s personal. Antisemitism touches real people, real families, and real lives. This PAC exists because silence is not an option.

Together, we can send a message that antisemitism — and all forms of hate — have no place in our politics or our country.
