Fighting Antisemitism. Defending Democracy.

We support candidates of any party who oppose antisemitism, reject political extremism, and strengthen America’s democratic values.

We are a federal political action committee dedicated to combating antisemitism and supporting responsible leadership.

We support Democratic, Republican, and Independent candidates based on values — not party — including opposition to hate, respect for democratic institutions, and support for strong U.S. alliances.

About Us

Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC is committed to confronting antisemitism in public life and supporting candidates who uphold pluralism, security, and democratic norms.
