Now is the time to elect candidates who will stand up to antisemitism.

Antisemitism is rising in our politics and communities. This PAC exists to make fighting antisemitism a defining political priority — through elections, leadership, and action.

A Growing Threat

Antisemitism is not a relic of history. It is present in our politics, schools, workplaces, and communities — and it demands political action.

Political Leadership

We support candidates who understand that confronting antisemitism is essential to democracy and moral leadership.

This Is Personal

Antisemitism impacts real people and real families. Silence is not enough. Elections are where accountability happens.

Our Mission

The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC exists to ensure that confronting antisemitism remains a national political priority rooted in America’s democratic values. We work to elect leaders willing to act — not just speak.
