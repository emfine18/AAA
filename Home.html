About Us

Americans for Tomorrow’s Future is a new political action committee dedicated to promoting America’s Global Leadership. We support the election of Democratic, Republican, and Independent candidates to federal office who advance America’s positive role in the world, including through support for America’s strategic alliance with Israel.

Americans for Tomorrow’s Future is deeply committed to America’s alliance with Israel because we believe the relationship provides extraordinary value to the United States, helps ensure national and global security and provides benefits to the entire world through advances in health care, environmental technology and water development.

At this time of hyper-partisanship, we place value on candidates willing to work in a bipartisan fashion to advance America’s global leadership and the U.S.-Israel relationship. We support or oppose candidates based on their policies, not their party.

Our contributors span the political spectrum, and many equally dedicate political donations to candidates on both sides of the aisle.
