Antisemitism is rising in America. Silence is no longer an option.

This PAC exists to make confronting antisemitism a political requirement — not a talking point, not a press release, and not something leaders can ignore when it’s inconvenient.

Antisemitism is not abstract. It shows up in classrooms, on campuses, in public office, and online. It shapes who feels safe, who feels targeted, and who is told — directly or indirectly — that they do not belong.

Democracy fails when hate goes unanswered. Elections are where accountability begins.

Antisemitism is a political issue

When elected leaders excuse, minimize, or ignore antisemitism, they create the conditions for it to grow.

Leadership requires action

Statements are not enough. Confronting antisemitism demands courage, clarity, and accountability.

Neutrality enables harm

Silence in the face of antisemitism is not neutrality — it is permission.

Antisemitism isn’t theoretical. It affects how people live, work, organize, and move through the world.

It affects parents deciding whether their kids are safe at school. It affects students wondering if they belong. It affects communities deciding whether anyone in power will show up for them.

Our Commitment

The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC exists to ensure that antisemitism is confronted with the seriousness it demands — in elections, in policy, and in leadership. We support candidates who understand that moral leadership must be reflected in political leadership.
