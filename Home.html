Our Mission

Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC is a federal political action committee dedicated to confronting antisemitism and supporting candidates who uphold democratic values, reject hate, and promote inclusive public policy.

We support leaders based on principles and values — not party — and stand against political extremism in all forms.

About Us

The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC works within the federal political system to support responsible leadership that rejects antisemitism, defends human dignity, and preserves the security of all communities.

Our contributors represent a broad cross-section of Americans who believe that hate has no place in public life.
