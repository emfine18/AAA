Our Mission

Antisemitism is a growing threat to Jewish communities and to democracy itself. The Alliance Against Antisemitism PAC works within the federal political system to support leaders who oppose hate and defend pluralism.

Key Priorities

Combat Hate Crimes

Support strong enforcement and legislation to protect targeted communities.

Reject Extremism

Hold elected officials accountable for rhetoric and policies that fuel hate.

Defend Democracy

Support free elections, civil rights, and constitutional norms.
